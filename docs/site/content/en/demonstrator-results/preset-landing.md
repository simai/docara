# Payments for your online store in one day

Accept cards and instant bank transfers with one API. Test mode is available
right after sign-up.

[Start for free](/en/demonstrator-results/preset-docs/)
:::

:::features
- **One request.** A payment is created with one API call, and Acme Pay shows the payment page itself.
- **Refunds.** Full and partial refunds from the dashboard or through the API.
- **Notifications.** The store learns about a payment at once, without polling the API.
:::
