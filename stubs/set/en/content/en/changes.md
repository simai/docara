## How to record changes

Write an entry when you change the set, not just before a release. Collect
new entries under "Upcoming" until the release; at release, give the
section its version number and date. In each entry, name the component, its
new version and what a person on the page will notice: new behaviour, a fix
or a change after which they must update their own code.
